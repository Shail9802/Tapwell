Put a little intention between you and your phone. Tapwell uses NFC tags to unlock distracting apps and help you keep up with everyday habits.

Download the Android APK ↗

Free to use. Works locally without an account.

01 / Focus

Make opening an app a choice.

Lock selected apps or YouTube Shorts. Scan your Focus tag for a timed session. Set daily limits and location-aware work breaks.

02 / Habits

A reminder you can act on.

Track water, stand breaks and custom routines. Choose intervals, repeat alerts and NFC confirmation. Log from resizable home-screen widgets, too.

03 / Your setup

Feel at home on your phone.

Material You colors follow your wallpaper. Optional Google account sync backs up core settings and NFC pairings in a private Google Drive app folder.

Your Google account. Your backup.

In the account-enabled app, sign in with Google and approve private Drive sync for that same account. Google handles sign-in; Tapwell never asks for your password. Users do not need to create a Cloud project or OAuth client.

Permissions, activity history, daily progress and active timers stay on each phone. See the privacy policy for what sync includes and how to delete a backup.
