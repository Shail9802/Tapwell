# Privacy operations

Owner and private privacy/grievance contact: Shail Shah, shailmshah.ss@gmail.com.

This is the operating procedure for Tapwell, not a claim of legal certification. Review territorial scope and applicable duties before entering additional markets or adding processing. Resolve questions of legal interpretation with qualified advice. Do not claim that a free or open-source app is automatically exempt.

## Requests

1. Receive requests privately. Acknowledge promptly, record only the request, receipt date, scope and outcome needed to handle it; do not copy health histories into the issue tracker.
2. Explain what Tapwell holds locally, in user-authorized Drive configuration and in optional health destinations. The developer has no remote database of users' journals and cannot access a user's private Drive by default.
3. Use proportionate verification only if needed. Do not ask for passwords, NFC unlock codes or government ID by default. Guide local users to the water journal view/JSON export, editable settings, clear-journal control, Android clear-storage control and receiving-app record controls. Clarify that sign-out, disconnect, revocation and deletion do different things.
4. Aim to resolve within one month or the shorter applicable deadline. Explain any lawful limitation/extension and complaints route. Do not falsely promise remote erasure of records managed by Google, Samsung or another device. Help the user stop all devices from recreating a deleted backup.
5. Delete request correspondence and attachments when no longer needed, subject to justified legal retention. Do not retain health attachments merely to prove a request occurred.

## Processing boundaries

- Local data supports user-selected focus/habit tools; app-private storage and Android screen lock are the baseline controls. Water event pruning occurs on new logs, not an exact timed purge.
- Google identity and Drive configuration are optional. Custom habit titles may reveal health information and saved work location is precise. Disclose these before sync. Drink-event history and water-export consent/status never enter Drive configuration sync.
- Health Connect export is opt-in, hydration-write only. Native Samsung export requires separate opt-in/approval, writes confirmed water and reads only own-ID matches for retry safety. No unrelated health access, advertising, developer health-data server or automatic backfill.
- One active export destination; changing it requires disconnect. Do not replay old destination queues into the new destination.
- Treat privacy contact mail and SDK partnership profile as separate processing from app water data. Do not publish the business address, phone or credentials in app source or support attachments.

## Release review

Check policy against implemented flows; test consent denial/revocation, disconnect, destination isolation, duplicate handling, local export/deletion and exclusion from Drive. Verify SDK notices/license and exact signing certificate. Record untested conditions honestly. Do not distribute the native Samsung build before approval. Reassess child audience, legal bases, sensitive processing, international transfers, rights fulfilment and any representation/DPO or reporting obligations where applicable; documentation alone does not resolve those questions.

## Incidents

Triage private reports, contain exposure, preserve minimum secure evidence, assess affected data/users/services and applicable reporting clocks immediately. Samsung's agreement can impose a one-business-day notification duty, while legal/other partner deadlines may differ. Coordinate fixes and notifications without posting personal records publicly. Document outcome and delete incident evidence when its justified retention ends.
